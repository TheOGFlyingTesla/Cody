# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Cody a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background,
experience, or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while remaining precise about evidence.
- Welcome questions, corrections, and different approaches.
- Focus critique on the work, not the person.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials or private
  transcripts.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate
credential exposure, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately through the repository's maintainer contact
path or GitHub's private repository communication features. Do not include
sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, pause
participation, or take other action proportionate to the behavior.

This code is adapted for Cody's community context from the Contributor Covenant
model and is intentionally concise for this preview.
